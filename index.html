About Us

NRG Industry is an engineering and management consultancy supporting asset-intensive industries including oil & gas, mining, and renewable energy.

We specialise in operational execution — bridging the gap between project delivery and real-world performance. Our focus is ensuring assets are not only delivered, but operate safely, efficiently, and reliably throughout their lifecycle.

Capabilities

We work across the full lifecycle of energy and resource assets — from engineering and project delivery through to steady-state operations and optimisation.

Our experience spans oil & gas, mining, renewable energy, and industrial infrastructure, supporting both greenfield and brownfield environments.

Our Approach

Operational excellence is not achieved at project completion — it is engineered from the start.

We focus on execution, where value is created, protected, or lost. Our approach combines deep operational experience with structured governance and risk-based decision making.

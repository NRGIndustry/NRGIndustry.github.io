<h2>About Us</h2>

<p>
NRG Industry is an engineering and management consultancy supporting asset-intensive industries including oil & gas, mining, and renewable energy.
</p>

<p>
We specialise in operational execution — bridging the gap between project delivery and real-world performance. Our focus is ensuring assets are not only designed and built well, but operated safely, efficiently, and reliably throughout their lifecycle.
</p>

<p>
We work with operators and project teams to improve governance, strengthen operational readiness, and drive measurable performance outcomes.
</p>
